# CAPTURE.md — Aetherhold catch-net

**Dump thoughts here. One line is enough. Don't structure at capture time.**

The rawest surface — the train-of-thought catch-net (distinct from `INBOX.md`,
the human↔agent decision channel). Capture by opening Claude Code on this repo
(remote is fine) and adding a line, or "capture: <thought>". Triage later, in
batch, never at capture time.

**Triage (scheduled):** each open item becomes one of — `→ idea` (to `ideas.md`)
· `→ task` (action, maybe `due:`) · `→ segment` (to a project plan) · `→ note`
(a fact → docs) · `→ done` · `→ drop`. At triage, also assign the **lane**
(ideation | research | feature | work | docs | learning) — it routes the item
to the right recipe and files its eventual retro in the right place.

## Entry format
```
- [ ] <thought, one line> {lane: <lane>?} {project: <name>?} {due: YYYY-MM-DD?}  (captured YYYY-MM-DD)
```
`lane` is optional at capture (zero friction), required at triage.
