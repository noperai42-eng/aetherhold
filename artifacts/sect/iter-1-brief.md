# Sect Iter 1 — Hollow mill slice

Additive mortal pocket on Aetherhold’s existing dual-view client. **Not a rewrite of the colony sim.** No claim of ship.

## Place

A cold mountain **hollow**: earth bowl, thatch cottages, a timber mill on a race. Palette is muted earth, wet wood, and high-country light. No neon qi, no spirit techniques.

## What landed

- Client **scene mode** (`src/client/scene/`, `src/client/worldkit/`). `src/sim` is untouched, so the eval fingerprint does not move.
- Procedural Three.js kit only: shared material roles wood, wet wood, thatch, stone, plaster, water, cloth, ground, mountain, foliage (10 ≤ 12).
- Mill with a **paddle wheel** and a **race**. Starts jammed. Clearing is a mortal job (gate-lever + haul).
- Two cameras on the same pocket: inhabit (human-height) and manager (oblique). `V` swaps them.

## Non-goals this slice

- Cultivation combat, meridians, or OP powers
- Sim jobs, power watts, or colony watermill changes
- Merge to main
